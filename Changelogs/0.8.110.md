# 0.8.110

## Fixes

- Fixed missing Objective-C classes, metaclasses, superclasses and ivar offsets in files linked with `-interposable`, such as the frameworks of iOS simulator runtimes.
- Superclasses defined in another image are now named in files without chained fixups, such as the frameworks of the iOS 15 simulator runtime.

## Compatibility

- Requires MachOKit 0.54.101 and MachOKitExtensions 1.2.0 or later.
